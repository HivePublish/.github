# Hive

Plan your content, publish across social platforms and stay connected to your audience in one workspace.

Hive Publish brings together social publishing, DM automations, Hive Bio, analytics and your connected accounts.

[Visit Hive](https://hivepublish.com) · [Developer documentation](https://hivepublish.com/developers) · [Official MCP connector](https://github.com/HivePublish/hive-mcp)

## Connect Hive to your AI assistant

Our [hive-mcp repository](https://github.com/HivePublish/hive-mcp) contains the connector configuration, Hive skill and setup guidance. It connects to the shared Hive MCP service; users sign in to Hive and grant access to their chosen workspace.

Available actions follow the user's permissions, subscription and connected accounts. Marketplace availability and approval are managed separately by each platform.
